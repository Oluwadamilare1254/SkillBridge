How it works
From zero to hired — step by step
1
Enroll in a track
Choose a skill area aligned to your goals. Pay once, access all modules, lessons, assignments, and resources inside.
2
Learn and build
Complete sequential lessons. Every module ends with a real assignment graded by your tutor — not just a quiz.
3
Pass the assessment
Sit a timed skills test. You need a passing score to earn your certificate. We mean it — no participation trophies here.
4
Connect to employers
Your verified profile — portfolio, assessment score, and certificate — is shown to employers actively hiring in your skill area.
